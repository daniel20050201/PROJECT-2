Who We Are?

DLO was founded by Daniel Sim Xuan Huan, Lau Zhin Thin, and Oon Chin Yong with the goal of spreading awareness about water conservation and sustainable usage.

Our Mission

To inspire individuals and communities to adopt water-saving practices and reduce wastage.

Our Vision

A future where clean water is accessible to all through responsible water management and conservation.

Our Goals

  • Encourage sustainable water habits at home and in industries
  • Support innovative water conservation projects
  • Advocate for policies that promote responsible water usage
